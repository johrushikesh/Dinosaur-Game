SKY DASH

Endless Runner

Swipe between lanes, jump and slide through a neon skyway. Grab coins and power-ups, chase near-misses, unlock skins, keep your daily streak alive.

DNA: Subway Surfers lanes · Jetpack Joyride power-ups · Monopoly GO! streaks · Fortnite missions

BLOCK REX

Block Puzzle

Drag blocks onto the 8×8 board and clear rows & columns. Chain combos for multipliers, beat the seeded Daily Challenge, unlock new themes.

DNA: Block Blast! core · Tetris clears · Candy Crush juice · Wordle daily
🚀👾

NOVA SWARM

You fire automatically — just keep moving. Survive escalating waves, level up and pick your build, take down bosses, unlock new pilots.

DNA: Vampire Survivors loop · Archero upgrades · Brawl Stars characters · Survivor.io bosses
